
# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Drosh is committed to maintaining an open, respectful, and constructive community.

We aim to provide a welcoming environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

All participants are expected to communicate professionally and treat others with respect.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Communicate constructively and professionally.
- Accept constructive criticism.
- Focus on technical discussions rather than personal disagreements.
- Respect different perspectives and experience levels.
- Provide helpful, actionable feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Collaborate in good faith.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Threats, intimidation, or deliberate hostility.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Trolling, persistent disruption, or bad-faith participation.
- Deliberately misleading or sabotaging other contributors.
- Any other conduct that creates a hostile or unsafe environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Drosh community spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories.
- Issues and pull requests.
- GitHub Discussions.
- Project-related communication channels.
- Other spaces where someone is officially representing Drosh.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainers.

Please include relevant details and, when possible, links or other context that help explain the situation.

Reports will be reviewed by the maintainers and handled as appropriate.

For security vulnerabilities, use the separate reporting process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers have the authority to determine appropriate responses to violations.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, enforcement actions may include:

1. A warning.
2. Removal of inappropriate content.
3. Temporary restrictions on participation.
4. Permanent exclusion from Drosh community spaces.

Maintainers may take appropriate action without following this order when necessary.

Enforcement decisions are made at the discretion of the project maintainers.
